ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Built for Windows.
Built to stay fast.

Base handles navigation. FAFS and FAStore keep the catalog and history. Recovery and FAAV act on that state without turning the filesystem path into a bottleneck.

  • 01No fake measurementsUnknown, stale and unavailable remain visible.
  • 02Filesystem-native identityStable IDs and ancestry matter more than path strings.
  • 03Recovery is separate from detectionContainment still works when restoration does not.
  • 04Local-first processingCore filesystem intelligence stays on the machine.
