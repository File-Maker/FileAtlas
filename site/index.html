ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Windows underneath.
Your data stays local.

Base provides the storage view. FAFS and FAStore maintain the local catalog and history. Upcoming recovery and FAAV components build on that foundation.

  • 01Clear measurement statesUnknown, stale and unavailable remain visible.
  • 02Filesystem-native identityStable IDs and ancestry matter more than path strings.
  • 03Recovery is separate from detectionThe upcoming design treats containment and restoration as separate capabilities.
  • 04Local-first processingCore filesystem intelligence stays on the machine.
